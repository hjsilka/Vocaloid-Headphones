I recreated the iconic headphones worn by Megurine Luka on the cover of the song Magnet. I upgraded the design of the headphones with a fully functional LED ring that lights up and reacts dynamically to music and a moving butterfly. Since they are intended to be used as a cosplay prop, they don't need to actually be functional and play music, but instead, they react to sound from the environment.

<p align="center">
In this repository, you will find my documentation of the process, a video demonstrating the final product, and the code.
